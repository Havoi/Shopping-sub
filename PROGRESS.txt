Correctness verification
The final image passed all 36 supplied fixtures through its normal entry
point with the supplied Docker runner. Both public answer tables matched
exactly, with zero missing rows, extra rows, differences, or duplicate keys.
The public result has 117038 user/day rows and 27400 campaign/day rows.

An independent Python implementation matched seeds 2, 17, and 42 at
30K events and 10K clicks each. An additional adversarial dataset covered
a long identity chain, an unused canonical ID, leap-day and midnight
sessions, invalid early duplicates, malformed quoting, CR bytes, and ties.

The final-scale result has 6648247 user/day rows and 28014 campaign/day
rows. Both tables total 10055848 sessions and 19223422711 revenue cents.
Schema, unique-key, purchasing-session, and attribution-total checks passed.
Large generated datasets have no supplied golden answers. Their checks
complement the exact comparisons on the public and independent datasets.

Implementation
Strict CSV and timestamp parsing.
Union-find identity resolution followed by integer person IDs.
Global event-ID deduplication before invalid or late records are removed.
Full short event IDs stored inline, with full borrowed values for long IDs.
Time-ordered session scans and seven-day campaign attribution windows.
Explicit Parquet schemas, DATE columns, and nullable top categories.

Performance work
Two early 60M runs timed out during deduplication at the local 6 GiB limit.
Integer person IDs reduced sorting costs. Storing short event IDs inline
removed scattered CSV reads during duplicate comparisons. Deduplication
then took about 3.4 seconds at final scale. The compact binary first
completed in 56.77 seconds, followed by the final-image run in 54.82 seconds.

Generation and packaging
The 60M input was generated with a scratch copy of the official generator
that writes shards incrementally to fit local RAM. Its seed-1 output
matched all 87 original-generator files byte for byte before use.
Input generation is outside the timed pipeline runs.
The final Docker image is 28808658 bytes, about 27.5 MiB.
Submission source and small fixtures are under 1 MB before Git metadata.
Fixture bytes are protected from Git newline conversion.
